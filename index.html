Rice Statistics for Africa — built on FAOSTAT and USDA PSD. Self-sufficiency indicators follow FAO (2001), Food Balance Sheets: A Handbook, as applied by Gassi, Gul & Cetin (2025), Rice Self-Sufficiency in Benin: Analysis and Forecasts, International Journal of Food and Agricultural Economics 13(1), 29–42.

Observed data, model forecasts and policy simulations are distinct kinds of statement and are labelled as such throughout. Scenario results are simulations under stated assumptions — not predictions, and not causal estimates of policy impact.
